# Potato Corner Promotion Management

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Marketing submits promotions; IT configures POS buttons; OP reviews work. Administrators manage access and integrations.

## Product Purpose

Coordinate promotion submissions, POS setup and status tracking without re-entering existing promotion details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing campaign saving and Microsoft login must remain independent of email setup. The Gmail connection uses one sender, `it@rocks-foods.com`. An implemented, default-disabled Co-Promotion pilot queues an initial reply and a single combined IT-plus-Interface reply in an explicitly approved test conversation. Verified identities, protected grants, per-button business records and explicit recipient confirmation are required. Live deployment, mailbox-owner consent and actual test sends remain separate approvals; no production delivery is implied.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the existing Potato Corner green theme and Thai/English language switching. New settings extend the current interface rather than redesigning it.

## Evidence on Hand

The approved connection design and implementation plan are in `docs/superpowers/`. Google client setup screenshots show Internal audience and Gmail scopes; live owner consent and deployment secrets remain unverified.
